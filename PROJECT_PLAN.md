# HealthTrust Watch – Project Plan

## Project Objective

The objective of HealthTrust Watch is to use Natural Language Processing (NLP) to identify and understand emerging privacy, trust, and reliability concerns in digital health application reviews.

## Primary Stakeholder

The primary stakeholder is a Digital Health Product Risk or Trust & Safety Manager who needs to monitor user feedback and identify concerns that may require further investigation.

## Planned Analysis

The project will follow Path B: Understanding/Extraction. Planned analysis will explore:

- Privacy and trust concerns in review text
- Differences across health applications and app types
- Relationships between identified concerns, sentiment, and star ratings
- Changes in privacy and trust concerns over time
- Potential early-warning signals based on unusual changes in specific concerns

## Expected Outcome

The project aims to develop an interpretable framework that helps product teams identify app-, issue-, and time-specific patterns that may warrant further investigation.
